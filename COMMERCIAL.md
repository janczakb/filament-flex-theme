## What is one domain?

A domain slot is a production **hostname**, not every page, panel, server, user or Composer installation.

- `example.com` and `www.example.com` count as one hostname.
- `admin.example.com`, `app.example.com` and `example.org` are separate production hostnames.
- Multiple panels and paths in one application at the same hostname do not consume additional slots.
- Replicas and multiple servers for the same permitted deployment do not consume additional slots.
- Private staging, local development, previews, CI and backups for your licensed application are included. A paid license is still required; this is not a free-use grant.
- A live customer or staff system is production even when called `staging`, `demo` or `test`.

A Single license can follow a genuine domain migration of the same application. It cannot be rotated across concurrently running sites or unrelated applications. An expired key may need assistance for a domain reassignment; it does not include a promise to operate activation infrastructure forever.
